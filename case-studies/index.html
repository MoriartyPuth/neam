Case studies

Neam finds people, organizations and places in Khmer news. Each case study looks at one part of how it works: what it does, why Neam needed it, how much it helped, and whose work it builds on. A plain explanation comes first, with technical detail underneath.

Reading each word from its meaningful piece

A third of Khmer words were labelled from a blank "▁" piece. Moving the label to the first piece with letters improved all three matched training runs, by 2.3 F1 on average.

Contents: an interactive view of the tokenizer's pieces, the fix and its code, the controlled test, the release history, and prior work.

How Neam reads a Khmer article

Hidden characters, articles longer than the model can read at once, and mapping every answer back to the exact characters. Fixing the last step alone raised validation F1 from 54.90 to 69.03 with the same model.

Contents: a hidden-character explorer, a window visualizer on an invented article, and two lessons about scoring.
