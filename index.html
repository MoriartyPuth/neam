How we keep score

How we built it

Eight decisions, and why each was necessary

Where are the words?

Cut the text into words first

foundation
Why it was necessary: the model labels one word at a time, and Khmer doesn't show where words end. Each word also remembers its exact position, so answers point back to the right spot.

Which piece should it look at?

Read each word from its meaningful part

+9.7biggest single step
Why it was necessary: the model splits words into pieces, and about a third of words start with a blank marker that looks the same for every word. The standard method asked the model to decide from that blank, like guessing a word from an empty tile.
✗ Standard method

✓ Neam

4
Names are known by their neighbours

Read long articles in overlapping windows

+5 to 6on organizations
Why it was necessary: the model reads at most about 80 words at a time. Cutting an article into separate chunks leaves words at the edges with context on one side only, and those were the names it missed.
✗ Separate chunks

The red word sits at a chunk edge: it sees only one side.

→
✓ Overlapping windows

Each word is read from the window where it sits most centrally.

Real example

Given one sentence alone, the model missed ក្រសួងធម្មការ និងសាសនា (Ministry of Cults and Religion). Reading it inside the full article, it found it.

5
One opinion is not enough

Ask seven models and average

steadierless luck involved
Why it was necessary: two models trained the same way can differ by up to 8.6 points, purely by chance. Averaging seven smooths out the unlucky ones.

6
A name can't start in the middle

Choose the best answer for the whole sentence

+2.5 to 3.7every model tested
Why it was necessary: deciding each word on its own often cut names short or produced impossible answers. Checking the whole sentence at once only allows names that start and end properly.
✗ Word by word
→ ភ្លើងទៀន ("candle-light": half the name)

✓ Whole sentence

7
Examples must agree

Write one rulebook and teach it

+5.4on unseen news
Why it was necessary: our examples labelled the same kind of phrase in different ways, so the model learned to guess. We wrote the rules down, fixed the examples, and added 64 practice sentences for the most confusing rule.
✗ Mixed examples

article B:ជនជាតិឥណ្ឌូនេស៊ី(not a name)

✓ One rule
"ជនជាតិ + country" describes people, not a place.
ជនជាតិឥណ្ឌូនេស៊ី ចំនួន ១០នាក់ … នៅខេត្តព្រះសីហនុ

Real output: the province is a place; the nationals are not.

8
Labelling is slow, so spend it well

Label the articles the model is unsure about

+5.2from 21 articles
Why it was necessary: every labelled article costs human time. Labelling articles the model already handles teaches it little. The articles where the seven models disagree are where it is learning.
Rank articles by disagreement illustration

Darker = models disagree more. Outlined = chosen for labelling.

→
✓ Label, check, retrain, repeat

Every label is reviewed by a person. Only positions are stored; the news text stays with its publisher.
